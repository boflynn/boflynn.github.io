My therapist told me that I need to be more accepting of my shortcomings and
that my ego gets in the way of me trying out new things. Turns out, if you think
you're hot shit, you don't like to keep proof around that there's a lot of stuff
you're not good at.

With that out of the way, here's some stuff that's not great.

## Elementle

 * [Site](https://boflynn.net/elementle)
 * [GitHub](https://github.com/boflynn/elementle)

Inspired by games like [Countryle](https://countryle.com/) and my son's growing
interest in the periodic table, this guessing game guides you towards the random
element based on its attributes compared to your guess, e.g. name, number,
phase.

## Tappy Plane

 * [Site](https://boflynn.net/tappy-plane)
 * [GitHub](https://github.com/boflynn/tappy-plane)

Mostly the output of a Zenva course on Godot with a few "improvements" while I
was going through the process. I want to clean this up and test out some
features to validate what I've learned.

This was also fun to set up the deployment pipeline to automatically publish the
Godot project to the web.

## Moment Tracker

 * [Site](https://boflynn.net/moment-tracker)
 * [GitHub](https://github.com/boflynn/moment-tracker)

A very simple one-page application to show countdowns to events, made via Google
Gemini. I'm quite happy with what it made; even the name is pleasant.
